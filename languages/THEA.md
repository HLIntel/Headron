# `languages/` — what this place is

the language packs — one guide, one operating card and one tool manifest per route

## A change here proves

- `contract`
- `own_enforcement`

## Traps already met here

Each one was committed in this repository at least once. `thea failures` has the full ledger.

- **a_gate_that_resolves_to_silence** — a full manifest. The role is filled in, the reviewer sees a tool, and the unrunnable gate and the passing gate print the same nothing
  - do: print every roster's count and refuse a zero — no targets, no files and no cases are findings, not passes
- **a_gate_that_aliases_another** — broad gate coverage, every name resolving
- **a_named_mechanism_that_does_not_exist** — a covered case, with a place named for it
