Canonical model-aware operating layer for Claude, Cursor, OpenAI/Codex, OpenCode, Hermes, VS Code, and generic LLM providers.

Routes cover all documented languages and their extensions. See integrations/MCP-LANGUAGE-MATRIX.md and integrations/MCP-PROFILES.md.
